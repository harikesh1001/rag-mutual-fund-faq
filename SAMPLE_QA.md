# Sample Q&A

Sample questions and answers demonstrating the facts-only behavior of FundFacts AI.

## 1. What is SBI Large Cap Fund?

**Answer:**

SBI Large Cap Fund is an open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large-cap stocks. It is offered by SBI Mutual Fund and managed by SBI Funds Management Ltd.

**Source:** SBI Large Cap Fund – KIM  
https://www.sbimf.com/docs/default-source/sif-forms/kim---sbi-large-cap-fund-%28formerly-known-as-bluechip-fund%29.pdf

## 2. What is the investment objective of SBI Large Cap Fund?

**Answer:**

The investment objective is to provide opportunities for long-term growth in capital through active management of investments in a diversified basket of large-cap equity stocks. The scheme does not guarantee that its investment objective will be achieved.

**Source:** SBI Large Cap Fund – KIM
